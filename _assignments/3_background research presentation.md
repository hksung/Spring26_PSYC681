## ✦ Guidelines

- This presentation introduces the background research relevant to your project topic.
- The main goal is to help your classmates understand the key studies that inform your project.
- Time limit: 12 minutes total (maximum 15 minutes)  
	- ~8 minutes presentation  
	- ~4 minutes Q&A  

- In the Project Proposal assignment, each group created an Annotated Bibliography (at least 3 sources). For this presentation, you should:
	- Introduce at least 3 sources closely related to your project
	- Explain the main ideas, findings, or experiments of each source
	- Discuss how these sources inform or motivate your project

- Possible elements to cover:
	- Research problem addressed in the paper  
	- Methods or approach used  
	- Key findings  
	- Why the study is relevant to your project
