## ✦ Guidelines

- While preparing for your background research presentation, you should also begin working on the first draft of your final project, which is called Midway report.
- For the final project, please use the **ACL (Association for Computational Linguistics) paper format** (template provided below). This format is commonly used for NLP and computational linguistics conferences.
- ACL submissions typically require choosing either a **short paper** or a **long paper** format.
- For this **midway report**, please include:
	1. **Introduction**
	2. **Background / Related Work**
- These sections do not need to be perfect at this stage, since you will have an opportunity to refine them later in the semester.

### Length guidelines

- If you plan to submit a **short paper** for the final project:  
  - Your midway report should be **no more than 1 page** (maximum **1.5 pages**).  
  - This allows you to reserve the remaining pages for your experiment and results later.

- If you plan to submit a **long paper** for the final project:  
  - Your midway report should be **no more than 2 pages**.

- These sections are the **minimum requirement**. If you would like to receive more feedback, you are welcome to include additional sections (e.g., methods or preliminary ideas). The goal of this assignment is to check your progress and provide early feedback on your project.

## ✦ Evaluation (7 points total)

1. **Introduction (2 pts)**: The research topic and motivation are clearly explained.
2. **Background / related work (3 pts)**: Relevant prior research is summarized and appropriately connected to the project.
3. **Connection to project idea (1 pts)**: The report clearly explains how the reviewed work motivates or informs the proposed project.
4. **Organization and formatting (1 pt)**: The report follows the ACL format and is well organized and readable.
